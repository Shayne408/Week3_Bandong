Customer service

Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Add candles to your cart, press Checkout, then fill in your details. We confirm every order by message.

Can I pick up or walk in?

Yes. Visit 281 Lower Cabinet Hill any day from 10 am to 5 pm.

How do I care for my candle?

Trim the wick to about 5 mm, burn 2 to 4 hours at a time, keep away from drafts and never leave a flame unattended.

I am sensitive to scents. What do you suggest?

Try our Unscented range: Glass, Pillar and Tealight candles.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Message us on Instagram, Facebook or TikTok with a photo and we will make it right.

Need a hand?

Our team is happy to help by message or in person, 10 am to 5 pm.
